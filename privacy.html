Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2, 2026

TL;DR: WebAsset Downloader collects ZERO data. Everything happens locally on your device.

Overview

WebAsset Downloader is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension operates entirely on your local device and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

Data Collection

We collect ZERO data.

Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Browsing history
  • Personal information
  • Usage analytics
  • Tracking cookies
  • Telemetry data

How the Extension Works

All asset discovery and processing happens locally on your device:

  • Website scanning occurs in your browser
  • File extraction is performed client-side
  • Downloads are saved directly to your computer
  • No data is sent to external servers

Permissions Explained

activeTab

  • Purpose: Access the current webpage you're viewing to scan for assets
  • Scope: Only active tab when you click the extension
  • Data Access: Reads page HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to find downloadable resources
  • Storage: Not stored; processed in real-time only

scripting

  • Purpose: Execute JavaScript to extract asset URLs from the page
  • Scope: Only when you initiate a scan
  • Data Access: Analyzes page structure to identify resources
  • Storage: Not stored

downloads

  • Purpose: Save discovered assets to your Downloads folder
  • Scope: Only files you choose to download
  • Data Access: Writes files to your local file system
  • Storage: Stored locally on your device only

host_permissions (<all_urls>)

  • Purpose: Allow extension to work on any website you visit
  • Scope: Required for asset discovery on any domain
  • Data Access: Only when you actively use the extension
  • Storage: Not stored

Third-Party Services

This extension does NOT use any third-party services:

  • No analytics (Google Analytics, etc.)
  • No error tracking
  • No advertising networks
  • No external APIs
  • No remote servers

Data Sharing

We do not share any data because we do not collect any data.

Children's Privacy

This extension does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You have complete control:

  • All data processing is local
  • You can uninstall the extension at any time
  • Uninstalling removes all extension data
  • Downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.
